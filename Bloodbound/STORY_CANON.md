# BLOODBOUND · Story canon

Shinegen Oda is an original fictional samurai and the last known survivor of his Oda household. The Demon Overlord destroyed that household to seize Ketsumei. A shrine legend establishes that distant Oda descendants may have survived beyond the hills.

## Ketsumei's curse

Ketsumei awakens when a chosen Oda heir falls with an unfinished oath of revenge. Accepting resurrection binds the heir's soul to the blade permanently. On each death the soul passes into **The Between**, a dark space between life and death. The gold and crimson gateways are distant glimpses of Heaven and Hell. Both are forever beyond the reach of a cursed bearer. The crimson thread pulls the soul directly back into the body while the oath remains unfinished.

Completing the oath destroys the bearer's body and soul. Their consciousness ceases to exist, with no passage into Heaven or Hell, reincarnation, surviving ghost, or further resurrection. The same fate applies to every Oda warrior who accepts the curse.

The Demon Overlord's defeat fulfills Shinegen's oath. His crimson thread vanishes and his soul breaks apart in place as his body dissolves. The original blade falls into the shrine stone. The sword and its curse survive. In the distant future an unidentified figure bearing the Oda emblem reaches for the grip. The scene cuts to black before the blade is drawn, followed by “ONE OATH ENDS.” and “ANOTHER AWAITS.” and the credits.

The morning voice, shrine inscription, and warning before the final duel foreshadow the cost. Voices and remembered images are echoes of the curse. Shinegen's last disappearance is final.
